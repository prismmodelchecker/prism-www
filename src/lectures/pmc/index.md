# Lectures - Probabilistic Model Checking

The following are slides for a lecture course on *Probabilistic Model Checking* as taught by [Dave Parker](http://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/David.Parker/) in the [Department of Computer Science](http://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/) at [Oxford University](http://www.ox.ac.uk/).

The course closely follows material from Chapter 10 of "[Principles of Model Checking](http://mitpress.mit.edu/catalog/item/default.asp?ttype=2&tid=11481)" by Christel Baier and Joost-Pieter Katoen (MIT Press) and "[Stochastic Model Checking](/bibitem/KNP07a/)" by Marta Kwiatkowska, Gethin Norman and David Parker. It is an extended version of (a subset of) [this course](/lectures/esslli10/) from ESSLLI'10 and [this course](/lectures/biss07/) from BISS'07.

The [tutorial](/tutorial/) for the probabilistic model checker [PRISM](/) provides a suitable accompanying practical course.
