# Sponsors

The development of PRISM began in the [School of Computer Science](http://www.cs.bham.ac.uk/) at the [University of Birmingham](http://www.bham.ac.uk/), and is now led from the [Department of Computer Science](http://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/) at the [University of Oxford](http://www.ox.ac.uk/).

::: logos
[![University of Birmingham](/images/bham_logo.png)](http://www.cs.bham.ac.uk/)
[![University of Oxford](/images/oxford_logo.png)](http://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/)
:::

The project has benefited from funding from: [EPSRC](http://www.epsrc.ac.uk/), the [European Research Council](http://erc.europa.eu/) (ERC), the [European Commission](http://ec.europa.eu/) (EC), the [British Council](http://www.britishcouncil.org/), the [DTI](http://www.dti.gov.uk/), [Microsoft Research Cambridge](http://research.microsoft.com/), [QinetiQ](http://www.qinetiq.com/), [DARPA](http://www.darpa.mil/) and [Google Summer of Code](http://code.google.com/soc/) (2013, 2014, 2016).
