# PRISM accepted for Google Summer of Code 2013

*April 2013*{.note}

We are delighted to announce that PRISM has been selected to participate in the 2013 [Google Summer of Code](http://code.google.com/soc/) scheme.

Google Summer of Code is a global scheme that pays students (at undergrad, MSc or PhD level) to contribute to selected open source software projects. Students are paired with a mentor at the project, contribute code over a three-month period in the summer, and get paid 5000 USD by Google. This is a great way for students to get experience of real-world software development and find out more about possibilities for further study and employment in areas related to their academic studies. See the [Google Summer of Code FAQ](http://www.google-melange.com/gsoc/document/show/gsoc_program/google/gsoc2013/help_page) for more details.

PRISM (see [www.prismmodelchecker.org](/)) is a software tool for modelling and studying the behaviour of real-life systems whose behaviour exhibits unpredictability or randomness. It can be used to analyse everything from the reliability of distributed filing systems, to the performance of a Bluetooth-enabled wireless device, to the safety of a car's airbag control system. PRISM has been used by researchers worldwide in fields as diverse as quantum cryptography, systems biology, computer security and robotics. Development of PRISM is currently split between the University of Birmingham and the University of Oxford.
