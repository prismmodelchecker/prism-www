# ESSAI'23 - Model Uncertainty in Sequential Decision-Making

We provide here material from the lecture course "[Model Uncertainty in Sequential Decision-Making](https://essai.si/model-uncertainty-in-sequential-decision-making/)" delivered at the 2023 [European Summer School on Artificial Intelligence (ESSAI)](https://essai.si/) by [Dave Parker](http://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/david.parker/home.html), [Bruno Lacerda](https://ori.ox.ac.uk/people/bruno-lacerda/) and [Nick Hawes](http://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~nickh/).

Here are slides from the 5 lectures:

-   [Lecture 1](/courses/essai23/essai23part1.pdf) (introduction and MDPs)
-   [Lecture 2](/courses/essai23/essai23part2.pdf) (stochastic games and uncertain MDPs)
-   [Lecture 3](/courses/essai23/essai23part3.pdf) (uncertain MDPs and interval MDPs)
-   [Lecture 4](/courses/essai23/essai23part4.pdf) (sampling-based uncertain MDPs)
-   [Lecture 5](/talks/essai23part5.pdf) (Bayes-adaptive MDPs)

Here are the [PRISM](/) files for the robot running example from lectures 1-3:

-   MDP robot model [robot-mdp.prism](/view/?f=/courses/essai23/examples/robot-mdp.prism) and properties [robot-mdp.props](/view/?f=/courses/essai23/examples/robot-mdp.props)
-   Interval MDP robot model [robot-imdp.prism](/view/?f=/courses/essai23/examples/robot-imdp.prism) and properties [robot-imdp.props](/view/?f=/courses/essai23/examples/robot-imdp.props)

See [here](/manual/ThePRISMLanguage/UncertainModels) and [here](/manual/PropertySpecification/UncertainModels) for more details about modelling uncertainty in PRISM.
