# [Probabilistic Model Checking Practicals](/courses/pmc1617/)

## Michaelmas Term 2016

* * *

These practical exercises are based on the [PRISM](/) tool. This is already installed on the lab machines in room T6.09. However, you will be expected to complete some of this work outside of the lab sessions. You will also need PRISM to complete the take-home assignment at the end of the course. Therefore, you will mostly likely wish to install a copy of PRISM on your own machine. Please contact the tutors of practicals [Min Wu](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/min.wu/) or [Gabriel Santos](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/gabriel.santos/) if you have any problems in this respect.

**Running PRISM in room T6.09:**  
You can run PRISM by just typing `xprism` at the command line.

**Installing PRISM on your own machine:**  
See [here](/courses/pmc1617/prism/).
