# [AIMS-QuantVer'15](/courses/aims1415/): Quantitative verification, part of the Systems Verification course of the AIMS CDT

The site contains various resources for the Quantitative Verification component of the [Systems Verification](http://aims.robots.ox.ac.uk/course/systems-verification/) course taught for the [AIMS](http://aims.robots.ox.ac.uk/courses/) CDT (Hilary Term 2015).

-   Slides for the lectures given by [Marta Kwiatkowska](http://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/marta.kwiatkowska/):
    
    -   [part 1](/courses/aims1415/marta-qver-p1.pdf) (Introduction + discrete-time Markov chains);
    -   [part 2](/courses/aims1415/marta-qver-p2.pdf) (Markov decision processes + strategies).
    
      
    
-   A [reference list](/lectures/references.pdf) for papers on probabilistic model checking, as cited in the lecture slides  
      
    
-   A [lab session](/courses/aims1415/lab/) based on the [PRISM](/) tool.

## Background reading

-   This [tutorial paper](/bibitem/FKNP11/) is a good source of background reading.  
      
    
-   The book [Principles of of Model Checking](http://mitpress.mit.edu/books/principles-model-checking) by Christel Baier and Joost-Pieter Katoen is recommended reading for the course.  
      
    
-   There are further tutorial papers listed [here](/publications/selected/).
