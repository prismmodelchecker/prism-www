# Probabilistic Model Checking practicals 2024–25

## Michaelmas Term 2024

## Practicals will be:

-   ### in room 379 on Wednesdays from 15:00 to 17:00 (weeks 4, 5, 7 and 8) for Group 1;
    
-   ### in room 379 on Thursdays from 15:00 to 17:00 (weeks 4, 5, 7 and 8) for Group 2.
    

* * *

These practical exercises are based on the [PRISM](/) tool. This is already installed on the lab machines. However, you will be expected to complete some of this work outside of the lab sessions. Therefore, you will mostly likely wish to install a copy of PRISM on your own machine. Please contact the tutors of practicals [Gabriel Santos](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/gabriel.santos/) or [Rajarshi Roy](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/rajarshi.roy/) if you have any problems in this respect.

**Running PRISM from lab machines:**  
You can run PRISM by just typing `xprism` at the command line. You can connect to a lab machine using ssh or remote desktop, e.g.: ssh -Y *user*@ecs.ox.ac.uk  
More information on remote access [here](https://wiki.cs.ox.ac.uk/support/RemoteAccess).

**Installing PRISM on your own machine:**  
See [here](/courses/pmc2425/prism/).

## Resources

The best source of information about PRISM is the online [manual](/manual/).

Outside of the lab sessions, general questions about PRISM (but not specifically about the exercises, please) can be sent to the [PRISM discussion group](http://groups.google.com/group/prismmodelchecker).
