## Practicals will be:

-   ### in room T6.09 on Mondays from 13:00 to 15:00 (weeks 4, 5, 7 and 8) for Group 1;

* * *

These practical exercises are based on the [PRISM](/) tool. This is already installed on the lab machines. However, you will be expected to complete some of this work outside of the lab sessions. Therefore, you will mostly likely wish to install a copy of PRISM on your own machine. Please contact the tutors of practicals [Emanuele La Malfa](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/emanuele.lamalfa/) or [Daqian Shao](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/daqian.shao/) if you have any problems in this respect.

**Running PRISM from lab machines:**  
You can run PRISM by just typing `xprism` at the command line. You can connect to a lab machine using ssh or remote desktop, e.g.: ssh -Y *user*@ecs.ox.ac.uk  
More information on remote access [here](https://wiki.cs.ox.ac.uk/support/RemoteAccess).

**Installing PRISM on your own machine:**  
See [here](/courses/pmc2223/prism/).

## Resources
